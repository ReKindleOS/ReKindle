If you cancel

Your files, photos, documents and notes stay stored. Cloud features pause while the membership is inactive, and everything comes back when you rejoin; the ReKindle+ apps return to preview mode. Nothing is deleted when a membership lapses.

Why it is paid

ReKindle is independent and ad-free. Mail sync, AI, cloud storage and photo processing cost money every month, and a membership is what keeps them running without putting ads or tracking on the dashboard. The free half of the app is not a trial.
