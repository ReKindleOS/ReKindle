Pixel tracing templates
=======================

Copyright (c) 2025 Ukiyo / ReKindle.

The 16 tracing shapes in this directory are original geometric pixel art
authored for ReKindle. They are not copied from any third-party icon pack.

They are released under CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication), so the
generated template data may be copied, modified and reused freely:

    https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The source of truth is scripts/pixel-template-art.js. js/pixel-templates.js is
generated from it deterministically by scripts/build-pixel-templates.js.
